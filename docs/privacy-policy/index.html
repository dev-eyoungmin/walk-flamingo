Wobbly Flamingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Wobbly Flamingo: Balance Game (also called Wobbly Flamingo or Wobby) handles information on Android and iOS. The developer is Young Min Lee (be.onn). Privacy contact: be.onn.corp@gmail.com.

1. No app account or developer-hosted user profile

You do not need to register or sign in to play. We do not operate an app account system or a developer-hosted database of player profiles. Gameplay and purchase-entitlement records are stored on your device. Third-party advertising and app-store services process data as described below.

3. Advertising and sharing

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Data sent directly from your device to Google's advertising services is covered by this policy even though it is not sent to our own server. Advertising-related data may be shared with Google and its advertising services for the purposes listed above. Ads and SDK processing depend on platform settings, consent where applicable, and service configuration. Declining access to an advertising identifier does not necessarily stop all other SDK data processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Purchase processing is governed by the relevant app-store provider's policies; see also Apple's privacy information.

4. Security

Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. App-store purchase communication uses the platform provider's secure services. No transmission or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data, purchase entitlements and recent transaction identifiers remain in app storage until changed, overwritten or removed. You can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove local records from that installation. Clearing local data does not erase purchase records held by Google Play or Apple, and purchases may be restored through the relevant store.

Google AdMob and the app-store providers retain data under their own policies and legal requirements. We do not set or control a fixed retention period for data held by those providers. Use their privacy controls for information they hold.

The app currently provides no account-creation system or account-based deletion process, and no in-app data-deletion request feature. For questions about this policy or how to use available local/platform privacy controls, contact be.onn.corp@gmail.com.

6. Your choices

You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through your device's privacy/advertising settings. On iOS, tracking permissions can be managed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking where applicable. Other device/SDK identifiers or contextual advertising data may still be processed. You can choose not to make purchases; basic play does not require an in-app purchase. Core gameplay can be played offline, while advertising and purchases need network access.

7. Audience

The app is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. It includes third-party advertising and optional in-app purchases. Contact us with any concern about a child's use or information.

8. Support and changes

If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond to your inquiry. Do not include passwords or full payment details. We may update this policy when app features or data practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
